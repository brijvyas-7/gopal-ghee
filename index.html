Our Story

Tradition You Can Taste.

Every batch begins with A2 milk from our own farmer's family cows, just outside Ahmedabad — collected fresh each morning, never stored for days.

The milk is set into curd overnight, hand-churned into butter using the traditional bilona technique, then slow-simmered over a low flame until it turns into golden, aromatic ghee.

No machines rush the process, and nothing is added to speed it up. It's slower, and it costs more — but it's the only way we know to make ghee that tastes like it used to, straight from a farmer's home to yours.
